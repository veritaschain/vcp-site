What is VeritasChain Protocol (VCP)?

VeritasChain Protocol (VCP) is an open standard specification for recording the "decision-making" and "execution results" of algorithmic and AI-driven trading — including AI model versions, decision factors, time-synchronized event lifecycles, and cryptographically hashed audit trails — in a tamper-evident, append-only and verifiable format. VCP provides a cryptographically secured chain of evidence, designed to support compliance efforts with international regulations including MiFID II, EU AI Act, and CAT (Consolidated Audit Trail), making it a valuable tool for RegTech, Audit, and Compliance. VCP verifies the integrity and attribution of recorded events — it does not certify the correctness or safety of the underlying AI or trading decisions.

VCP also records:

In addition to the order and execution data that FIX messages carry, VCP defines records for:

  • AI model versions and decision factors
  • Active risk limits and triggered controls
  • Time-synchronized (PTP / NTP) timestamps
  • Cryptographically sealed audit trails

FIX standardises the messages exchanged between counterparties; it does not define a record of a system's internal decision factors or risk state.
This makes VCP relevant to evidence requirements under MiFID II RTS 25, EU AI Act Article 12, and next-generation RegTech.

Why VCP Matters to Regulators

  • Reconstructing algorithmic decision-making
  • Verifying timestamp integrity
  • Detecting tampering, and omission after anchoring
  • Producing evidence relevant to EU AI Act Article 12 logging obligations
  • Verifying best-execution evidence

Why Now?

VSO's position is that oversight of algorithmic and AI-driven trading should rest on records that can be verified independently. The rules below require record-keeping, event logging or clock synchronisation; none of them mandates cryptographic audit trails or refers to VCP.

EU Clock Synchronization (formerly MiFID II RTS 25)

Requires business clocks traceable to UTC, with specified accuracy, for trading venues and their members and participants in the EU (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1155, Arts. 11–16; replaced RTS 25 on 2 March 2026)

EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

Requires automatic event logging (Art. 12) and human oversight (Art. 14) for high-risk AI systems; Annex III obligations apply from 2 December 2027

CAT Rule 613 (Ongoing)

U.S. SEC's Consolidated Audit Trail requires granular trade lifecycle tracking

VCP provides a single open standard that produces evidence relevant to all these requirements, reducing fragmentation across jurisdictions — by design.

Key Features

Tamper-Evident & Verifiable

Tamper-evident audit trails using Merkle trees (RFC 6962 domain-separated hashing) and external anchoring; hash chains are optional

Crypto Agility

Ed25519 by default, upgradeable to post-quantum algorithms including ML-DSA (FIPS 204)

Each signature carries its algorithm identifier, so algorithms can be changed. Post-quantum signatures are EXPERIMENTAL in VCP v1.2.0 GA (tag v1.2.0, commit b23acb266f88df81d960d36d58fb64ceaa0dd5ed; GA merged 2026-10-08 JST; fix merged 2026-10-09 JST) and are not a certification requirement. This is the VCP release status, separate from VAP v1.2.0 Draft 3.

Regulatory Evidence

Designed to produce evidence relevant to MiFID II RTS 25, EU AI Act, GDPR, CAT Rule 613, and international reporting

Multi-Tier Support

Compliance tiers for HFT (Platinum), Institutional (Gold), and Retail (Silver).

Note: Silver Tier relies on system time (Best-effort) and delegated signatures. It provides transparency, not regulatory-grade evidence.

FIX Protocol Integration

Side-car integration with existing FIX engines — no change to trading gateways required

Open Standard

Fully open specification with public reference implementations. Designed for long-term interoperability across brokers, exchanges, and regulators.

Verifiable Completeness

Split-View Attack and Omission Attack resistance (v1.1). Designed so that removing events from an anchored batch, or showing different records to different parties, is detectable.

AI Governance Logging

Records algorithm identity and version, a model hash, decision factors and approval records (VCP-GOV). Where a system is classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act, such records are evidence relevant to Article 12 record-keeping; algorithmic trading is not listed in Annex III.

Technology Stack

1. Event ID
UUIDv7 (RFC 9562, time-ordered, v4 fallback)
2. Signature
Ed25519 / ECDSA / ML-DSA (PQC)
3. Verification
Merkle Tree + Chain Validation
4. Timestamp
PTP (IEEE 1588-2019) / NTP (Chrony)
Platinum: <1µs | Gold: <1ms
Silver: Best-effort (system time; no guaranteed precision)
5. Storage
SBE or FlatBuffers (Platinum) / JSON (Gold, Silver)
Kernel bypass, RDMA and zero-copy are techniques the specification names for Platinum implementations
6. Integration

VAP Framework

Verifiable AI Provenance — Domain Profiles

Industry-specific profiles for cryptographically verifiable AI audit evidence

VAP (Verifiable AI Provenance Framework) — which VSO describes as "the flight recorder for AI" — is the cross-domain upper-level metaframework (current: v1.2.0 Draft 3) that defines the structural requirements for cryptographically verifiable AI decision provenance. Its scope is deliberately strict: domains where system failure could cause serious, irreversible harm to human life, societal infrastructure, or democratic institutions. Finance, healthcare, transportation, energy, and public policy are VAP's mandatory application domains; content/creative AI and capture provenance are served by released profiles. VAP itself is not an implementation — domain profiles apply its requirements to a sector. Status in the VAP registry (§4.5.2): VCP v1.2 Released; CAP v1.0 Released; CPP v1.4 Released; OAP v0.1.1 Working Draft; MAP v0.1.2 Working Draft; PAP Partial Draft (Track B v0.2.0); IAP Announced; DVP, AAP and EIP Planned (no specification document exists for IAP, DVP, AAP or EIP). VAP and VCP: common questions

What is VAP?

VAP (Verifiable AI Provenance Framework) defines shared requirements for verifiable records of AI decisions and actions. The published framework is v1.2.0 Draft 3; it is a draft, not a certification of an AI system.

How are VAP and VCP related?

VAP sets cross-domain requirements. VeritasChain Protocol (VCP) is the financial domain profile for algorithmic and AI-driven trading records. Other profiles address different domains.

What can these records prove?

Cryptographic evidence helps verify the integrity and provenance of recorded events. It does not establish that an AI decision was correct, safe or legally compliant.

Where can I start?

Read the VAP framework for the shared requirements. For trading systems, explore the VCP specification and the verification demo using sample data.
