Our Mission

"Encoding Trust in the Algorithmic Age"

We work toward transparency and verifiability of financial-market records through cryptographic protocols, driving the paradigm shift from "Trust Me" to "Verify Me." In modern financial markets dominated by high-frequency trading and AI-driven algorithmic trading, our mission is to make records of trading decisions and executions independently verifiable: a third party can check whether a record was altered, or omitted after anchoring, without relying on the record holder's word. A verifiable record does not establish that the underlying decision was correct.

Distributed Operations

VSO operates as a geographically distributed organization. We do not store or process audit data centrally—records stay with the implementing organization, and any third party can verify them from the records, their Merkle proofs and external anchors (for example an RFC 3161 time-stamping authority or a public blockchain). The secretariat functions are lightweight and relocatable, with no physical security requirements for data custody.
