Separate publication from adoption

VAP v1.2.0 remains Draft 3. VCP v1.2.0 GA describes specification publication, not production readiness or full VAP conformance. Merging research into main does not adopt new requirements.

VAP’s known issues include unpublished committee membership and decision records, the status of CHANGE-001 and the migration-period start. This directory does not resolve them.

Registry status is not document availability

OAP, MAP, PAP Track B and the DAP/SMP cross-cutting capabilities have registry entries, but their texts are not published in VSO repositories. IAP, DVP, AAP and EIP have no published specification texts either.

Use the VAP overview for domain introductions and the VAP specification page for the authoritative source and digest.

Pin the evaluation context

  1. Select a purpose and record the source commit, dependencies, inputs and trust configuration.
  2. Distinguish fresh execution from rechecking saved historical evidence.
  3. An expected negative verdict can make a test match; that does not make the evidence pass.
  4. Retain failures, incomplete runs, environment differences and support provided. Obtain execution agreement separately from publication permissions.
