    ANNOUNCEMENT October 21, 2026

    VSO Publishes a VAP Application Example: Verifiable Evidence for AI-Assisted Security Research

    On October 21, 2026, VSO published “Verifiable Evidence for AI-Assisted Security Research”, a VAP application example, in English and Japanese. It is a design proposal and is informative and non-normative: it sets no requirements and does not establish a new profile. The page explains how observation records under OAP (Observed Artifact Provenance, a domain profile) and delegation records under DAP (Delegated Access Provenance, a cross-cutting capability) could be combined so that a third party can check from evidence how AI-assisted security research reached a finding: who delegated what, which rules applied, what was actually retrieved, and how it was assessed. OAP and DAP are Working Drafts registered in VAP v1.2.0 Draft 3. Because their texts are not posted in VSO’s public repositories, the page describes them only as far as the VAP text does and marks its own proposals separately. It also states what the design alone does not guarantee: that observations are accurate, that a vulnerability exists, that the research was authorized or lawful, or that no unrecorded action took place. No implementation of this design has been released.

    ANNOUNCEMENT September 28, 2026

    VSO Publishes VAP Framework Specification v1.2.0 (Draft 3) on GitHub

    On September 28, 2026, VSO published the Verifiable AI Provenance Framework (VAP) Specification v1.2.0, Draft 3, on GitHub. VAP is a metaframework: it defines the common requirements that domain profiles inherit, so that records of AI decisions are auditable, attributable and, at anchor granularity, completeness-verifiable after the fact. It does not prevent, block or intercept any AI behavior. v1.2 makes external anchoring mandatory at every conformance level and adds the Completeness Invariant (INT-008): omission of events after anchoring must be detectable by a third party at batch granularity. Under §1.6, conformance does not constitute compliance with any law or regulation and "does not warrant the correctness, fairness, or safety of the underlying AI decisions — only the integrity, completeness (at anchor granularity), and attributability of their records." The draft is pre-release; it has not been adopted by, and has no standing within, the IETF, ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, ITU-T or any other established standards body. Current status: zero external implementations of VAP or of any VAP profile and zero Evidence Packs accepted in any proceeding. Commercial disclosure update (24 October 2026): the earlier contract figure is withdrawn pending primary-record verification; this does not establish zero contracts. Details The linked text includes the October 6 corrections (see above).

    VSO Establishes an AI Research Office in Hokuto, Yamanashi

    Effective July 6, 2026, VeritasChain Standards Organization (VSO) has established an AI research office in Hokuto City, Yamanashi Prefecture, Japan — on the southern foothills of the Yatsugatake mountains, about two hours from central Tokyo. The office belongs to VSO, the standards-development organization, not to VeritasChain Co., Ltd., the commercial entity. Every output feeds open specifications.

    ANNOUNCEMENT May 31, 2026

    VCP v1.2 Release Candidate Now Available — English, Japanese & Chinese

    The VeritasChain Protocol (VCP) v1.2 Release Candidate is now available at github.com/veritaschain/vcp-spec under spec/v1.2/, in English, Japanese, and Chinese. v1.2 is a protocol-compatible, certification-stricter enrichment of v1.1 with zero breaking changes — all v1.0 and v1.1 events and anchors remain valid and verifiable, and the v1.1 hard deadlines (Policy Identification 2026-03-25; Silver external anchoring 2026-06-25) are preserved unchanged. Key additions: strengthened VCP-RECOVERY operational constraints; an ERASURE event type reconciling the GDPR right to erasure (Article 17) with append-only audit trails via crypto-shredding; a version compatibility matrix; SCITT alignment fields; and cross-party (multi-actor) VCP-XREF. The release continues v1.1's alignment with EU AI Act record-keeping (Articles 12, 19, 26, 72) and MiFID II/MiFIR audit-trail requirements. Post-quantum signatures (ML-DSA / FN-DSA) advance to EXPERIMENTAL while Ed25519 remains the default. This is a Release Candidate for public review, not a GA release.

    Update: VCP v1.2.0 is General Availability (tag v1.2.0, commit b23acb266f88df81d960d36d58fb64ceaa0dd5ed; GA merged 2026-10-08 JST; fix merged 2026-10-09 JST). This is the VCP release status, separate from VAP v1.2.0 Draft 3.
