Bounded signature-verification correction — VAP #68

Both implementations explicitly reject small-order public keys and signature R points, noncanonical point encodings and scalars at or above the subgroup order before backend verification. The observed acceptance required the caller to substitute role trust keys; retaining the original trusted keys rejected the altered pack. Backend behavior differed by runtime. This is a local experimental input policy, not full prime-subgroup validation or a new VAP normative requirement. The recorded suite has 41 test methods (32 existing and 9 additional security methods); parameterized inputs are not a separate additive total. The 11 original vector/manifest files remain unchanged.

VAP documentation and change-process proposals — #66, #67, #69

Session/token validity is separated from permission intervals and revocation evidence. Source-monitoring notes distinguish reported proposals from adopted rules. Updated scope notes distinguish evidence verification from preventive control; LAP remains an external, unregistered proposal. Change-process and CHANGE-002 candidate inventories are unadopted drafts: formal identifiers, decisions and missing normative text/vectors remain unresolved. The IETF next-draft crosswalk is a proposal, not a submission or a published new revision. No public-review clock or committee approval is asserted.
