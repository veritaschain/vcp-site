VSO · 2026-11-25 · DOCUMENTATION & SOURCE REVIEW

VSO updates SCITT/VCP references, activity records, incident reviews and VeraSnap Android guidance

This dated notice brings together four updates already merged into VSO’s public repositories. Published drafts, individual mailing-list participation, source reviews, product notices and first-party CI remain separate kinds of evidence.

VAP remains v1.2.0 Draft 3. Experimental extensions and proposed repairs remain unadopted; independent third-party reproduction has not been established. Individual Internet-Drafts and mailing-list participation do not establish IETF Working Group adoption, endorsement, certification or production safety.

1. SCITT/VCP -07 is a published individual draft

draft-kamimura-scitt-vcp-07, dated 24 November 2026, is now published in the IETF Datatracker. It is no longer just an unsubmitted editor candidate; it remains an individual work in progress, not an IETF-adopted standard.

The revision corrects the SCRAPI description and examples and limits the profile’s use of Receipts to inclusion. It draws no conclusion from a Receipt’s tree size or leaf index. The entry rule, trusted service and keys, the handling of missing or contradictory inputs, and the scope of the AnchorRecord signature remain open issues. Posting the draft adopts no change to VCP requirements.

The VAP repository and organization profile now reference -07. The VAP framework draft remains -03; its pending review against the AnchorRecord issue is not presented as complete. Posted-draft record · VAP reference update

2. Forty individual SCITT messages, with their context

The SCITT mailing-list activity record lists 40 messages through 24 November, with links to the public archive and notes on earlier terminology and claims. The count is a count of messages, not endorsements, adopted proposals or independent reviews.

The record includes both -07 announcements sent on 24 November and explains their relationship; the second message does not change the draft. Individual participation is kept distinct from Working Group adoption. Historical messages remain available with current explanatory notes.

3. Seventy-eight library records: additions, follow-ups and corrections

The incident library now contains 78 records. This is a mixed source-review collection, including advisories and studies, not a count of 78 independently proven autonomous-AI incidents or demonstrations that VAP would have prevented harm.

  • CERT-In advisory CIAD-2026-0062: the advisory reports abuse of business records and approval relationships leading to unauthorised payments. Separately, it reports observations of AI-driven agentic offensive frameworks. The record does not establish that AI carried out the unauthorised payments. Victim records and restricted technical material were not inspected.
  • European Commission / EU Academy: the 23 November official account confirms a leak of EU Academy users’ names and email addresses in the same campaign. The exact scale remains under investigation. This is a follow-up, not a new incident count.
  • Korea Consumer Agency study: the corrected record states that, in the described test, Gemini and Perplexity did not ask about age, underlying conditions or current medication before giving drug information. Survey respondents’ self-reports do not establish a causal link to a particular model response.
  • Bell Canada: a dated follow-up records the wider description of personal and business customer data, attributing it to Bell’s statements carried by the reporting. The word “agentic” is not proof of AI involvement; the model, tool and degree of autonomy remain unverified.

VSO’s interpretation: authorisation, underlying business records, observation boundaries and custody must be examined separately. A tamper-evident record does not establish that a payment was legitimate. VAP is not a general-purpose payment-control or intrusion-prevention system; the payment-evidence proposal is unadopted. Official accounts, reporting and VSO’s analysis remain separately identified in each source record.

4. VeraSnap Android 1.6.1: a rollout notice with version limits

The VeraSnap technical page carries the dated 21 November Android 1.6.1 announcement. It describes a staged Google Play rollout, not confirmed availability on every device. This version does not include video capture, C2PA export, QR-code scanning or screen detection.

The public notice is not evidence of an independently reproduced cross-platform test. Check the current store listing, platform differences and business-use terms before an evaluation. Google Play · Business and insurer information

Main, fixed distributions and historical records remain distinct

No additional SDK or Explorer repository change was found in the review window beginning 23 November at 22:04 JST. This notice does not announce another SDK or Explorer release. The earlier SDK 125-test fixed-release record and 186-test main record remain historical snapshots.

The fixed VCP v1.2.0 tag resolves to b23acb2, the research kit to b1af0dc, and vap-interop-demo-v0.2.0 to cbd4502. There is no new fixed ZIP in this update. Previously recorded main-only repairs are not silently applied to those archives.
