Focus

Three questions guide the review.

A bounded workflow makes permissions, observed actions and evidence requirements concrete.

Authorization

Who was allowed to act?

Permissions, delegation, scope and revocation: identify the authority behind an action and the limits of that authority.

Execution

What actually happened?

Compare approved requests with observed tool actions and outcomes. Separate an instruction, an execution attempt and a confirmed result.

Evidence

What can be checked?

Assess integrity, provenance, missing observations, secrets handling and reproducibility. Record what remains unknown.

AI security reviews

Review an AI agent's authority, actions and evidence.

AI providers and governance teams can discuss a review of a bounded AI feature or agent workflow with Tokachi Kamimura. Architecture and code review, with agreed controlled testing, support findings linked to evidence and explicit unresolved questions.

Review question 01

Did the observed action match the permission?

Examine how permissions, delegation and revocation relate to execution records in a workflow that uses external tools.

Review question 02

What supports the AI's claim that a task is complete?

Separate the AI response, an attempted action and an observed result, then identify the evidence available to check the outcome.

Review question 03

What is missing from the audit trail?

Review integrity, provenance, gaps and secrets handling to identify what the records support and what additional evidence is needed.

Agree the scope and deliverables first.

Discuss the architecture, code and records to examine, the tests to perform, the evidence accompanying findings and how unresolved questions will be documented. Scope, deliverables, fees and timing are agreed before work begins. VAP adoption is not a prerequisite.

Research status and limits

VAP v1.2.0 remains Draft 3. Experimental extensions remain unadopted, and independent third-party reproduction has not been established. VSO-operated CI and internal synthetic tests are not independent reproduction. Record integrity does not establish truth, safety or legal compliance, recover unobserved events, or replace runtime controls.

Questions

Before discussing a technical review.

What AI security questions can I discuss with Tokachi Kamimura?

Reviews focus on authorization, tool execution and audit evidence for a bounded AI feature or agent workflow. They can cover architecture and code review, agreed testing, findings linked to evidence and explicit unresolved questions.

Can I discuss a system that does not use VAP or VCP?

Yes. Reviews can examine existing workflows and records without requiring adoption of VAP or VCP.

What should I include in the first inquiry?

Describe the workflow, the question you need to resolve, your intended deliverables and your timeframe. Start with a non-confidential summary without credentials or personal data. Required materials and how to share them are discussed together with the scope.

How do public research and commercial reviews relate?

VSO is a specification initiative operated by VeritasChain Co., Ltd., which provides commercial reviews. The publications and verification limits describe the research status. Technical reviews are not legal advice or conformity certification.
