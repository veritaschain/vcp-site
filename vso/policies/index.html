Official Statement

Statement on Neutrality, Operating Structure, and Non-Endorsement

Establishing Trust in Algorithmic Trading Through Transparency and Accountability

Our Mission

VeritasChain Standards Organization (VSO), operated by VeritasChain Co., Ltd., works to promote transparency, fairness, and security in algorithmic trading across global financial markets, guided by the mission: "Encoding Trust in the Algorithmic Age."

At the core of our work is the development, maintenance, and promotion of the VeritasChain Protocol (VCP) standard. To fulfill this role, VSO aims for vendor neutrality through public procedures and conflict-of-interest management, alongside its published non-endorsement policy. This statement clarifies our commitment to these fundamental principles and outlines important policies regarding the VC-Certified scheme.

1. Vendor-neutrality aim and public procedures

VSO is operated by VeritasChain Co., Ltd. It aims for vendor neutrality through public procedures and conflict-of-interest management. These are stated aims and policies; their publication does not establish structural neutrality or independence from its operator.

Vendor-neutrality aim

VSO aims to develop specifications that serve market participants without favoring a particular technology vendor, financial institution, trading platform, or national interest. The design aim is to avoid vendor lock-in and support the health of the financial ecosystem.

Objective Governance

VSO's governance aim is to enable transparent input from financial industry practitioners, regulatory compliance experts, academic researchers, and independent technical specialists. This describes intended participation, not verified committee membership or a claim that a committee with that composition is currently operating. The published CAP governance document provides a profile-level governance example, including roles and review procedures.

2. Operating Structure and Revenue Transparency

VSO is operated by VeritasChain Co., Ltd., a Japanese kabushiki kaisha. VSO does not describe itself as a non-profit organization. Published materials include specifications under CC BY 4.0, the scheme's requirement for conformity assessment by independent Conformity Assessment Bodies (CABs), and the non-endorsement policy in Section 3. These are part of the stated approach to vendor neutrality; the current CAB registration status is described in Section 3.

Revenue Transparency

The following are potential funding categories, not a statement of current income or available services:

  • Certification-program fees contemplated by the scheme (VC-Certified), subject to its governance and independent-CAB requirements
  • Technical training and workshops
  • Specification documents and developer resources
  • Donations and grants from industry partners

The funding aim is to support VCP development, maintenance, and dissemination. This page does not establish income received or that all income has been reinvested. Earlier commercial figures remain withdrawn pending verification; this is not a finding of zero income.

Operating Entity

VSO is operated by VeritasChain Co., Ltd., a stock company (kabushiki kaisha). Vendor neutrality is an aim for this company-operated initiative, pursued through the public procedures and conflict-of-interest management described on this page.

3. Non-Endorsement Policy

Certification Does Not Imply Endorsement

Important Principle

"VC-Certified" is a CAB assessment of technical conformance to VCP. It is not a compliance finding and not a product or service recommendation.

What Certification Means

The scheme requires CABs to be independent of the scheme owner. No independent CAB is currently registered; the only registered body, VeritasChain Co., Ltd., operates VSO and is not independent (see the CAB registry). VSO does not review submissions or issue certifications. The former application procedure in Conformance Guide §14 is historical and not in effect; see the current certification status. Under the published scheme, a CAB assessment would address whether a system or algorithm meets the technical requirements defined in the applicable version of the VCP Specification (current: v1.2), including:

  • Cryptographic signature validity (Ed25519; ML-DSA/Dilithium is EXPERIMENTAL in v1.2)
  • Timestamp accuracy (clock synchronization per tier: PTP for Platinum, NTP for Gold, best-effort for Silver)
  • Data structure integrity (UUIDv7, JSON Schema compliance)
  • Tamper-evidence of audit trails (Merkle Tree verification)

What Certification Does NOT Mean

Certification does not guarantee or recommend the following:

  • Investment Performance: Algorithm profitability or trading strategy excellence
  • Business Reliability: Company financial health or management ethics
  • Regulatory Compliance: Compliance with national financial regulations (MiFID II, SEC Rules, etc.)
  • Security Guarantee: Complete resistance to cyber attacks

VSO does not commercially recommend or promote products or services that have obtained VC-Certified badges. Users must properly understand the scope of certification and select/use products at their own responsibility.

4. Conflict of Interest Management

Disclosure Obligations

Under this policy, individuals serving as VSO board members, technical committee members, or certification auditors are required to disclose potential conflicts of interest (employment relationships with financial institutions, stock holdings, consulting contracts, etc.) in advance and are excluded from deliberations and votes on relevant cases.

Commercial Relationship Limits

VSO does not establish direct commercial relationships (e.g., consulting, joint development, marketing partnerships) with applicant companies beyond certification program review fees. This is a policy restriction, not a statement that VSO currently accepts certification applications or collects review fees.

5. Transparency and Accountability

Open Source Commitment

VCP specifications are published under CC BY 4.0. Reference code that VSO publishes on GitHub (for example vcp-sdk-spec and vcp-explorer-api, Apache 2.0) carries the open-source license stated in each repository. vcp-sdk v0.1.0, a pre-release offline core for VCP v1.2 written by VeritasChain Co., Ltd. and published in the same GitHub organization under Apache 2.0, is neither an implementation of vcp-sdk-spec nor a conformance test suite. SDKs implementing vcp-sdk-spec and an automated conformance test suite have not yet been published. Anyone can freely access, implement, and propose improvements to the published materials.

Annual Reporting

Annual financial and activity reporting is an intended transparency practice, covering use of funds, certification-program status, and progress in standardization activities. This policy statement is not confirmation that annual financial reports or certification statistics have been published. Published activity updates and dated corrections are available separately.

Conclusion: As a Foundation of Trust

VSO aims for vendor neutrality through public procedures and conflict-of-interest management, and maintains its published non-endorsement policy. These are stated aims and policies for building trust in financial markets of the algorithmic age.

Under the name "Veritas" (truth), we aim to combine technical rigor with ethical integrity and support a fair and transparent environment for all market participants.

This statement sets out VSO's aims and declared policies for stakeholders.
