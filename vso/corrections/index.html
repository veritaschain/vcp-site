Corrections log

VSO corrects its public statements by adding dated records rather than silently rewriting them. This page lists corrections to the specifications, repository READMEs and this website, newest first. Each entry links to its change record. Dated announcements and blog posts are kept as records of what was published at the time; where they are affected, they carry a note.

  1. 2026-10-28

    Independence, “mathematically unrecoverable”, production wording and RFC 6962 claims corrected

    The legal page's “Jurisdictional Independence” card is now “Jurisdictional Neutrality” and describes a design aim. The VAP-AT pages no longer say the separation model ensures independence: the only registered CAB is not independent of the scheme owner. The VSO policy page is retitled “Neutrality, Operating Structure and Non-Endorsement”, no longer lists independence among VSO's principles, describes VC-Certified as a CAB assessment of technical conformance rather than proof of compliance, and states the CAB's status; the Chinese IAP page no longer says certification is handled by independent bodies, and the CAB registry states technical competence as a requirement. Crypto-shredding is no longer described as making data “mathematically unrecoverable” or “unrecoverable”. The VCC, What is VCP and Explorer API pages no longer claim RFC 6962 compliance in any language. The English and Chinese VC-Certified FAQ privacy answer now matches the Japanese one. Dated announcements, blog posts and the 26 October announcement keep their text and carry a dated correction or note, including the home page cards that called first-party proofs of concept production-grade. A Chinese home page card that translated “production-like” as “production” was corrected.

    Records: vcp-site #156

  2. 2026-10-28

    Company founding date removed; CAB status shown on application pages; VeraSnap no longer described as CPP-compliant

    From 20–21 October 2026 the company, clarification, press and VSO pages had given a founding date for VeritasChain Co., Ltd. That date has not been confirmed against the commercial registry certificate, so it has been removed from the text and structured data until it is. The VSO activity-start date is unchanged. The VC-Certified application pages now state, next to the fees, that the only registered CAB is VeritasChain Co., Ltd. and is not independent of the scheme owner. The VeraSnap pages no longer call the app CPP-compliant: it is a first-party app implementing CPP v1.4, no independent conformance assessment has been performed, and the CPP v1.4 mapping to VAP v1.2 has not been published.

    Records: vcp-site #154

  3. 2026-10-26

    Follow-up to the 25 October alignment: neutrality wording, CAB milestone and FAQ translations

    The 25 October entry said the legal page no longer called VSO independent. At that time its section 3 was still titled “VSO Independence Policy”. That section is now the “VSO Neutrality Policy”, points to VSO's published neutrality mechanisms and has a “Role Statement”, and the VC-Certified FAQ's structured data in English, Japanese and Chinese no longer describes VSO as an independent body. The VAP-AT roadmap lists approval of the first CAB independent of the scheme owner as a planned milestone (a VSO-approved CAB already exists and is not independent), and its national-accreditation layer is marked as planned. The Japanese and Chinese FAQ pages no longer say that crypto-shredding makes data mathematically unrecoverable. Statements that separating roles prevents “pay-to-pass” outcomes now say the separation is intended to prevent them. The FAQ describes certification as a CAB assessment of technical conformance rather than third-party validation of technical compliance, and Chinese pages describe VC-Certified as a conformance certification (符合性认证). The IAP status uses one term per language. The 25 October entry is kept as published.

    Records: vcp-site #129

  4. 2026-10-26

    RTA verification scope, selected records and review intake clarified

    Updated the English, Japanese and Chinese RTA announcement and entry points to distinguish first-party CI from independent reproduction, selected original JSON copies from full artifact retention, and RTA review from the separate VAP OS-gate kit. Corrected the verification FAQ and Silver-tier reference description to match the actual legacy verifier; dated historical announcements retain their original text with a clarification. Experimental extensions remain unadopted and independent reproduction is not established. No normative requirement, implementation, signed evidence, fixed distribution or K3 material is changed.

    Records: VCP RTA / VSO RTA PR #17 RTA PR #18

  5. 2026-10-26

    Research, SDK availability and unresolved specification issues distinguished

    Added a 26 October research update in English, Japanese and Chinese, with the reviewed VCP PR #16 results and their limits. Clarified that vcp-sdk v0.1.0 is first-party pre-release code on GitHub, not a PyPI/npm distribution or an implementation of vcp-sdk-spec. Current specifications, experimental candidate schemas and the earlier fixed VAP reproduction kit remain separate. Unadopted status and the absence of independent third-party reproduction are retained. The website also incorporated the cross-language corrections recorded on 25 October; this does not adopt normative repairs or claim full conformance.

    Records: Research update VCP PR #16 Site PR #116

  6. 2026-10-25

    English, Japanese and Chinese pages brought into line

    Corrections already made in one language were carried over to the others, and remaining standing pages were aligned with the VAP v1.2 registry and current disclosures. Conformity Assessment Bodies are described as VSO-approved, and CAB independence as a requirement that the only registered CAB, which operates VSO, does not meet; the legal page no longer calls VSO independent. Superseded Internet-Draft revisions were replaced by the current ones. The VCP Explorer is described as a demo with sample data, and the zero-knowledge-proof, “military-grade”, “mathematically verifiable” and crisis-prevention claims were removed. VAP pages describe PrevHash as optional and ML-DSA as experimental, list all eight domains in Chinese, carry the implementation-status disclosure, and label VAP-GAM a concept rather than a VSO profile. The 24 October 2026 integrity-review notice was added to further VCP pages. Dated announcements and posts were not rewritten; a note was added only where another language version already had it.

    Records: Site changes

  7. 2026-10-25

    VeraSnap is VeritasChain Co., Ltd.'s app, not VSO's

    The 13 April 2026 announcement called VeraSnap “our production cryptographic evidence capture app”. VeraSnap is an app of VeritasChain Co., Ltd., the company that operates VSO, not a VSO specification, and its availability in app stores is not a claim of conformance to VAP or CPP. A dated note was added under the announcement (English and Chinese); the announcement text is kept as published.

    Records: vcp-site #111

  8. 2026-10-24

    Commercial contract figure withdrawn

    The previously published figure of ten paid service contracts with European organisations, held by VeritasChain Co., Ltd., has not been substantiated through reconciliation with primary records. It was withdrawn from the VAP, VCP, CAP and CPP repositories and this site pending that review. This is not a finding that there were no contracts, and no replacement figure is asserted.

    Records: Dated correction and counting criteria vap-spec #15 vap-spec #20 vcp-site #100 vcp-spec #5 cap-spec #2 cpp-spec #1

  9. 2026-10-24

    Regulator-submission figures under review

    The homepage figure of 67 regulatory authorities in 50 jurisdictions is no longer presented as a current count. The list included entries that are not regulatory authorities, a placeholder, a duplicate and an authority that no longer exists. Earlier dated announcements with regulator counts are kept, with a note that the totals remain under review.

    Records: vcp-site #102 vcp-site #104

  10. 2026-10-24

    VCP alignment with VAP v1.2 under review

    VAP §10.4 no longer records VCP v1.1 and v1.2 as fully conformant to VAP v1.2. The VCP v1.2 Merkle construction and audit-path examples conflict with the specification's own RFC 6962 requirement, and the root-only AnchorRecord signature does not authenticate the attached count, endpoint or policy metadata. Two integrity-layer drafts are published as unadopted discussion documents; they are not an issued erratum.

    Records: vap-spec #18 vap-spec #19 vcp-spec #5 vcp-spec #3 vcp-spec #4

  11. 2026-10-24

    VCP status and cross-party wording recorded as known issues

    The VCP v1.2 known-issues lists now record that §1.1 calls VCP "a global standard specification" and says it "establishes truth", and that §5.6 (VCP-XREF) uses guarantee and dispute-prevention language. The specification text is unchanged; these are errata candidates.

    Records: vcp-spec #7

  12. 2026-10-24

    Company-specific proposal pages removed

    Pages addressed to named companies were removed from this site. Some described exclusive positions under the VC-Certified scheme and interpretation of the standard by an assessment body; they were not descriptions of the scheme. A test now fails if such wording returns.

    Records: vcp-site #105

  13. 2026-10-24

    "Approved", not "accredited", Conformity Assessment Bodies

    Under ISO/IEC 17000:2020, accreditation is a third-party attestation related to a conformity assessment body. VSO's designation of a CAB under the VC-Certified scheme is an approval, and the site now says so. The only listed CAB provides VSO's operating base and is not independent of the scheme owner.

    Records: vcp-site #106

  14. 2026-10-24

    Mission wording aligned with VAP §1.6

    The company and exchange pages no longer say that VCP establishes "mathematically verifiable truth" or "cryptographically verifiable truth". They now describe third-party verification of whether a record was altered or omitted after anchoring; a verifiable record does not establish that a decision was correct.

    Records: vcp-site #104

  15. 2026-10-22

    Unverified SDK installation instructions removed

    Installation commands for package names that VSO has not published (npm @veritaschain/sdk and PyPI veritaschain, which returned 404) were removed from the organisation profile.

    Records: Organisation profile change
