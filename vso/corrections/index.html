VCP / VSO · CORRECTIONS · Last updated 2026-11-03

Corrections log

VSO corrects its public statements by adding dated records rather than silently rewriting them. This page lists corrections to the specifications, repository READMEs and this website, newest first. Each entry links to its change record. Dated announcements and blog posts are kept as records of what was published at the time; where they are affected, they carry a note.

  1. 2026-11-03

    Compiled website styles, script integrity and CSP migration

    The website change replaces the Tailwind Play CDN in 302 HTML pages and six source templates with a checked-in stylesheet that preserves each original configuration and namespaces generated animations. The VCC demo precompiles its JSX, removes runtime Babel, and builds the missing QR library locally from its pinned version. QR canvas sizing uses an integer module grid that fits the modal; certificate data is unchanged. Versioned external scripts and the legacy lazy-image fallback carry verified integrity hashes. Generated per-document CSP permits reviewed script and handler hashes without script unsafe-inline or unsafe-eval; existing inline styles remain allowed. Generators and offline browser checks cover the resulting output and essential interactions. Google's live analytics loader remains a documented, unpinned SRI exception: it loads only after the existing explicit opt-in, with advertising consent denied. The exception means the request to attach SRI to every external script is not fully closed. The linked source record documents the implementation and verification scope.

    Records: Website source changes and validation Compiled CSS design and validation

A status-check date records inspection of linked documents, not fresh conformance validation. Each PR shows its review and merge state.

  5. 2026-11-02

    Verification and collection boundaries corrected in implementation PRs

    Implementation PRs distinguish unsupported or missing trust checks (INDETERMINATE) from detected contradictions (INVALID/FAILED); local hashes are not authenticated verification. IBKR/FIX current checksum inventories are explicitly unsigned, and original metadata/manifests and fixed evidence remain available as historical records. Unsubstantiated production/HSM assertions are withdrawn without inventing provenance. Supervisor rejects unsupported JWT/mTLS at startup and does not issue legal-presumption results for unchecked timestamps. TradingView authenticates writes and retains failed/in-flight anchor batches; its and cTrader’s preserved odd-node tree construction is explicitly documented as differing from RFC 6962/9162. Payout output calls public prefixes demo tags rather than signatures. Cloudflare uses Web Crypto HMAC for secret comparison. A separate EVIDECT change proposes explicit opt-in for homepage analytics, privacy disclosures and removal of internal SEO material. Notification/sales separation and observation or suppression policy changes remain in a separate review draft; no email-suppression operation is asserted. The SDK installer gate requires reviewed bytes and fails closed while the approved digest is absent. PRs identify remaining checks and whether changes are merged.

  9. 2026-10-28

    Independence, “mathematically unrecoverable”, production wording and RFC 6962 claims corrected

    The legal page's “Jurisdictional Independence” card is now “Jurisdictional Neutrality” and describes a design aim. The VAP-AT pages no longer say the separation model ensures independence: the only registered CAB is not independent of the scheme owner. The VSO policy page is retitled “Neutrality, Operating Structure and Non-Endorsement”, no longer lists independence among VSO's principles, describes VC-Certified as a CAB assessment of technical conformance rather than proof of compliance, and states the CAB's status; the Chinese IAP page no longer says certification is handled by independent bodies, and the CAB registry states technical competence as a requirement. Crypto-shredding is no longer described as making data “mathematically unrecoverable” or “unrecoverable”. The VCC, What is VCP and Explorer API pages no longer claim RFC 6962 compliance in any language. The English and Chinese VC-Certified FAQ privacy answer now matches the Japanese one. Dated announcements, blog posts and the 26 October announcement keep their text and carry a dated correction or note, including the home page cards that called first-party proofs of concept production-grade. A Chinese home page card that translated “production-like” as “production” was corrected.

    Records: vcp-site #156

  10. 2026-10-28

    Company founding date removed; CAB status shown on application pages; VeraSnap no longer described as CPP-compliant

    From 20–21 October 2026 the company, clarification, press and VSO pages had given a founding date for VeritasChain Co., Ltd.     The 25 October entry said the legal page no longer called VSO independent. At that time its section 3 was still titled “VSO Independence Policy”. That section is now the “VSO Neutrality Policy”, points to VSO's published neutrality mechanisms and has a “Role Statement”, and the VC-Certified FAQ's structured data in English, Japanese and Chinese no longer describes VSO as an independent body. The IAP status uses one term per language. The 25 October entry is kept as published.

  21. 2026-10-24

    "Approved", not "accredited", Conformity Assessment Bodies

    Under ISO/IEC 17000:2020, accreditation is a third-party attestation related to a conformity assessment body. VSO's designation of a CAB under the VC-Certified scheme is an approval, and the site now says so. The only listed CAB provides VSO's operating base and is not independent of the scheme owner.
