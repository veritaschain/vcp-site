What is VC-Certified?

VC-Certified (VCP Conformance Certification) is a certification program where VSO-accredited Conformity Assessment Bodies (CABs) verify and certify that your trading system, algorithm, or platform conforms to the rigorous technical specifications of the VeritasChain Protocol (VCP) v1.1. The certification scheme is developed and governed by the VeritasChain Standards Organization (VSO), an independent standards body.

This certification is fundamentally different from traditional "financial audits" or "operational audits." The CAB technically verifies through automated test suites whether the data generated by your system is signed, tamper-evident, and meets the time-synchronization and numerical-precision requirements of its tier.

"Verify, Don't Trust."

We don't say "trust us." We say "verify us."

VC-Certified is the technical passport that enables that verification.

Three Fundamental "Veritas" Made Verifiable by Technology

Obtaining VC-Certified certification means making the following three elements cryptographically verifiable to customers, regulators, and the market

① Detectability of Fraud

"Hindsight Trading" Made Detectable

In VCP-compliant systems, each recorded trading event is assigned a time-ordered UUID v7, hashed, and included in a Merkle tree whose root is externally anchored (hash-chain linking is optional since VCP v1.1). After anchoring, retroactive deletion or modification of the anchored data is detectable—even when made by database administrators.

This makes changes to anchored records that would "cancel inconvenient trades" or "retroactively invalidate profits" technically detectable.

Cryptographic tamper-evidence

② Radical Transparency

Breaking Free from Black Boxes

AI and algorithmic decision-making processes (VCP-GOV) and risk parameters at that moment (VCP-RISK) are recorded and signed.

Customers and auditors who are given the records can inspect not just the resulting prices, but the recorded decision factors and the risk parameters in force at that time. The records show what the system logged, not whether a decision was correct. The public Explorer is a demo with sample data.

Recorded decision context

③ Tamper-Evidence & Integrity

String-Encoded Values, Signed and Merkle-Anchored

Eliminates financial data's greatest enemy—"floating-point errors (IEEE 754 problem)"—through string-based strict numerical representation.

Furthermore, Ed25519 digital signatures and Merkle tree (RFC 6962 domain-separated hashing) technology make data integrity cryptographically verifiable.

Standard cryptography: Ed25519 signatures, SHA-256, Merkle trees

Why VC-Certified Matters

In an era where AI and algorithms execute trillions of dollars in trades, accountability depends on records that others can check. VC-Certified attests that the records a system produces conform to the VCP specification, so that auditors and counterparties can verify those records independently. It does not attest that the system's decisions are correct, fair or safe.
