VeritasChain Protocol (VCP)

The World's First Open Cryptographic Audit Standard for Algorithmic Trading

What is VCP?

VeritasChain Protocol (VCP) is an open cryptographic standard that creates tamper-evident, end-to-end verifiable audit trails for AI-driven and algorithmic trading systems. VCP replaces trust-based oversight with mathematically verifiable transparency, enabling independent verification without requiring access to proprietary systems.

Key Innovation: VCP uses cryptographic hash chains (SHA-256) and chronologically-ordered UUIDv7 timestamps to create tamper-evident, append-only records that any third party can verify—from regulators to auditors to market participants—without trusting the record keeper.

Why This Matters Now

  • Trust Crisis in Algorithmic Trading: Recent scandals involving retail brokers, prop firms, and exchanges have eroded market confidence
  • Regulatory Pressure: MiFID II, SEC Rule 15c3-5, and FINRA 3110 demand stronger algorithmic oversight
  • AI Complexity: Black-box AI trading models make traditional audits insufficient
  • Global Market Need: No existing standard provides cryptographic-grade transparency for algorithmic systems

Technical Specifications

  • Hash Algorithm: SHA-256
  • Timestamp Format: ISO 8601 + int64 nanoseconds; event IDs are UUIDv7 (RFC 9562)
  • Architecture: Merkle-tree hash chain
  • Verification: Independent third-party
  • Data Format: Structured JSON events
  • License: CC BY 4.0 (Open Standard)

Certification Tiers

  • Silver: Retail / MT4-MT5: best-effort clock, anchoring every 24 hours
  • Gold: Prop / institutional: NTP (<1ms), anchoring every hour
  • Platinum: HFT / exchange: PTPv2 (<1µs), anchoring every 10 minutes

Progressive compliance framework allows organizations to adopt VCP incrementally based on their needs.

Launch Date
November 25, 2025
Protocol Version
VCP v1.1 (v1.2 Release Candidate available)
Standards Body
VeritasChain Standards Organization (VSO)
Target Markets
Brokers, Prop Firms, Exchanges, Hedge Funds

Use Cases

  • Retail Brokers: Make fair execution verifiable and stop-loss hunting detectable
  • Prop Trading Firms: Demonstrate transparent evaluation and payout systems
  • Cryptocurrency Exchanges: Verify order matching and make front-running detectable
  • Hedge Funds: Provide auditable compliance for algorithmic strategies
  • Regulators: Enable efficient oversight without system access

Regulatory Alignment

VCP produces evidence relevant to existing financial regulations (conformance does not constitute legal compliance): MiFID II (EU algorithmic trading requirements), SEC Rule 15c3-5 (Market Access Rule), FINRA 3110 (Supervision requirements), and emerging AI governance frameworks. ISO/TC 68 (Financial Services) is a potential future standardization pathway (no submission has been made).

Availability

Reference Implementation: Reference repositories are published at github.com/veritaschain (for example vcp-rta-reference); SDK source code is not yet public
