Three legal AI workflows

The proposed profile covers everyday points where AI participates in legal work.

01

Legal queries

Connect a submitted question with the response or other recorded outcome.

02

Document drafting

Follow a drafting request through generation and subsequent review.

03

Fact checking

Keep the verification request and the reported findings connected.

What the evidence records

A useful audit trail connects the work, the people and the supporting records. LAP describes how these connections can be examined within a declared scope.

Proposal scope and event design: Internet-Draft -05, §§4–18.

  • Inputs and outcomes

    The recorded context, actor, action and result.

  • Human review

    Who reviewed an output, when, and what decision was recorded.

  • Record integrity

    Signatures, linked records, Merkle commitments and external anchors.

  • Evidence handling

    Evidence Packs, retention, erasure records and legal hold controls.
